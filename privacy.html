Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains what data the Sawir app collects, how we use it and the choices you have. Sawir helps rice farmers manage water in their fields with a water sensor, a water gate and a hub.

Sawir is made by Sawir Corp (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions about your data, email us at sawircorp@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: you sign in with Apple. We receive your name and the email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email.
  • Field data: water levels, gate activity, plot names and boundaries, planting dates, crop stages and your watering plan.
  • Device data: the status, battery and signal of your hub, water sensor and gate.
  • Location: the location of your plots and devices, which you mark on the map. While you use the app, your phone’s location is used to show your position and help you place devices. We don’t track your location in the background.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a push token for your phone so we can send alerts about your field.

Permissions on your phone

  • Bluetooth and local network: to find your hub and connect it securely during setup. The Wi-Fi name and password you enter are sent to your hub so it can get online.
  • Camera: to scan the QR code on your hub. We don’t save photos.
  • Location (while using the app): as described above.
  • Notifications: to alert you about your field.

You can change these permissions at any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

How we use your data

  • To run your watering plan and open or close your water gate.
  • To suggest your plot boundary and track your crop stage.
  • To send you notifications about your field, such as a stuck gate or a crop stage check.
  • To show your water saved and your estimated methane reduction.
  • To improve Sawir. For this we only use data that doesn’t identify you.

We don’t use your data for advertising, and the app doesn’t include third-party analytics or tracking.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your data. We only share it with:

  • Service providers that help us run Sawir, such as cloud hosting, under agreements that protect your data.
  • Apple, to sign you in and to deliver notifications.
  • Partners in a carbon programme, only if you choose to join.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and field data while your account is active. Resolved notifications are deleted automatically after 7 days. When your account is deleted, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your plots at any time in My Plots.
  • Turn notifications off in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing sawircorp@gmail.com.
  • Delete your account and its data: email sawircorp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, and we’ll delete it within 30 days.

Security

We protect your data in transit and limit who can access it. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

Children

Sawir is made for farmers and isn’t intended for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we’ll let you know in the app. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
